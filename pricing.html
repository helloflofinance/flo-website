How a number gets set

No estimates by email.

Scoping a build off a two-line message is how people end up over-charged or under-delivered. Three steps instead.

1

Discovery call

Thirty minutes on where your time and money actually go. You leave with a plan whether or not we work together — that part's free and it isn't a pitch.

2

A number in writing

A fixed scope and a fixed price, sent as a document you can sit with. One number, what's included, what isn't. No hourly meter running.

3

Build, then hand over

flo builds it, wires it into your day-to-day, and teaches your team to run it. You own the system and the source — it's not rented back to you.
