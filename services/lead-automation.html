Where it leaks

The leak is almost never the leads.

Most small operators don't have a traffic problem. They have a response-time problem. Somebody calls during a job, leaves no voicemail, and books with whoever answered first.

The same leak shows up three more times. An enquiry that sat in an inbox overnight. A quote nobody followed up on. A booking that never got a reminder and turned into a no-show.

None of that needs a bigger team. It needs a system that does the predictable part the moment it happens, every time, without anybody remembering to.

Straight answers

Four things people ask.

How long does an automation build take?

Most first builds land inside two to three weeks from the discovery call. Scope drives it more than anything else: one workflow is quick, rewiring intake across a whole business takes longer. You get the timeline in writing before anything starts.

Do I have to change my phone number or my calendar?

No. flo builds around the number, calendar and inbox you already use. Making you migrate tools is how these projects stall.

What happens if the automation gets something wrong?

Every flow has a human override and a visible log, so you can see what fired and when. Nothing is a black box you can't inspect or switch off.

Do I own it afterwards?

Yes. You own the system and the accounts it runs on. flo hands it over and teaches your team to run it.
