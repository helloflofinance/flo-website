Fair questions

The ones worth asking.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

A chatbot starts cold every time and has no access to your files, your pricing or your history. These builds are narrow systems wired into your real material, with a defined job, a defined output format and a review step before anything leaves the building.

Where does my data go?

That gets scoped on the call and written into the build, including which model is used and what it is allowed to see. If a workflow shouldn't touch an outside service, it doesn't.

Does it send things without me seeing them?

Not unless you decide it should. The default is draft-and-approve for anything a client or a dollar touches.

What if it gets something wrong?

Outputs are reviewable and every run is logged. Narrow scope is the main defence: a system with one job and your own material to work from is far easier to check than a general-purpose chatbot.
